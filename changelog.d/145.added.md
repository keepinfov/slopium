  scope it stands in runs what it deferred before it releases what it owns,
  inner scopes first, and the nearest function is the one it leaves — inside a
  `lambda`, the `lambda`. A binding may move out through a guard's `return`
  and still be used on the path that answers. It is written where statements
  go — a body, a branch, an arm, a loop body — and refused in a value being
  built or under a call's arguments, and a function may not be named `return`,
  because every call would read as the form first.
