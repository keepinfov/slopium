# Builder baseline — 2026-10-26

Issue #153 separates builder construction, owned finalization and checksum.
This baseline selects the next experiment; it includes no new builder
optimization. The original kernel remains unchanged.

## Profiling evidence

The development shell has no `perf` executable. Its existing Valgrind 3.27.1
provides Callgrind and Memcheck, used in separate non-timed runs of the original
kernel with the fixed compiler. Callgrind recorded 216742938 instruction
references: 48.45% directly in `sl_rt_list_push`, 8.12% in
`core:builder:write-u64`, and 5.72% in `core:builder:write-byte`.
Instruction shares are not native wall-time shares.

The call graph counted 827778 list pushes, 50000 list constructions (one builder
and 49999 temporary digit lists), and 788890 runtime String byte reads (250000
short-piece bytes and 538890 checksum bytes). Memcheck recorded 240111 heap
allocations/frees, 25381506 total allocated bytes, no live blocks and no errors.
Total allocated bytes are not peak RSS. The profile and controlled interval
measurements both identify append/formatting as the first throughput target.

## Next experiments and numerical goals

1. Prioritize #155's integer formatting without a temporary list. Repeating the
   original control must remove the 49999 digit-list constructions, cut total
   heap allocation calls by at least 60%, improve the integer-only append median
   by at least 25%, and improve the original control wall median by at least
   10% in two independent paired runs. These are goals to verify, not achieved
   results or correctness-CI thresholds.
2. Keep #156 deferred initially: its checksum phase is measurable, but the
   first throughput work targets the much larger append interval. Reconsider
   it after append changes if checksum becomes a material share. General
   #90/#91/#142/#143 work is not a blanket prerequisite.
3. Use #157 to compare ownership-safe storage alternatives. After a separately
   approved representation decision, target useful storage of one byte per
   logical byte plus bounded padding and documented geometric spare capacity.
   Target original-control RSS at most 5120 KiB and the 4 MiB piece matrix at
   most 16384 KiB under these conditions. The current matrix is about 38 MiB.
4. Recheck every piece/document row after a change. Investigate repeatable
   median regressions above 3% in paired runs, especially five-byte appends.
   Report misses honestly; preserve all bytes, checked failures and ownership
   gates. Refresh this baseline after #151 and #154 land.
